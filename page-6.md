# Phase 1: Dungeon

Each round starts with the Dungeon Phase, where you will choose a dungeon and explore it by drawing chips from your Satchel and placing them strategically to solve adjacency puzzles. You start on the top floor, descending lower when you fill all the spaces on a floor, pulling and placing chips until your Satchel is empty or you choose to stop.

Once your dungeon is complete, move to the [Resolution phase](phase-2-resolution.md) to determine the effect of all the chips you played.

---

## Anatomy of a Dungeon

Each dungeon has **floors** (rows of spaces), **features** (that look like chips), and **obstacles** (which look like rubble). The different parts of the dungeon are explained in the diagram below.

![Anatomy of a dungeon](.gitbook/assets/anatomy-of-a-dungeon.png)

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| **Spaces** | Are divided by pillars. Each floor is made up of 6 spaces. |
| **Empty spaces** | Do not have a feature or obstacle, and chips may be played to them. Some have flavorful illustrations like torches or bones. Cool, but no gameplay effect. |
| **Dungeon Features** | Look like chips, and have the same effect as if a chip was placed there. **Only features on floors your Hero has reached affect you.** |
| **Mandatory Features** | Have a **red ring** around them. Chips may not be played to their space. |
| **Optional Features** | Have a **white ring** around them. Chips may be played to their space, replacing the effect with the played chip. |
| **Obstacles** | Prevent you from playing chips to that space. Ranged effects like armor, wands, and bows do not pass through **obstacles**. |
| **Floor** | A single row of the dungeon map. The floor with your Hero figure next to it is your **current floor**. |
| **Dungeon Entrance** | Maps show an entrance at the top and have 3 floors. |
| **Dungeon Depths** | Maps have 2 floors and no entrance. |

> When adding a Dungeon Depths map, you may place it directly below your Dungeon Entrance or shift it one square to the right or left.

---

## Step 1: Choose a Dungeon Map

Each player will start with their own Dungeon Entrance map. Each player should:

1. Draw a Dungeon Entrance map from the **bottom** of the stack.
2. Look at both sides. Place the map with the side of their choice face-up in front of them.
3. Place their Hero figure next to the top **floor** of their dungeon map.

Once all players have placed their Dungeon Entrance, play begins!
