# PHASE 6: VILLAGE

---

| **PHASE 6: VILLAGE** | **SHOP ACTIONS** |
|:---|:---|
| **Beginning with the starting player and proceeding clockwise, players take turns shopping. On each turn a player may buy a new chip from an open shop or visit one of the specialty shops. This phase ends when all players have taken 3 turns.** | ## SHOP ACTIONS |
| ## CHIP SHOPS | ### VISITING A SPECIALTY SHOP |
| There are 4 different chip shops in the village—two tier 1 shops, a tier 2 shop, and a tier 3 shop. The tier 3 shop begins the game closed, and opens in round 2. The shops offer the matching tier of chips. Players may not look at the face-down side of the chips in shops. | You may visit one of the other three shops instead of purchasing a chip. To do so, pay any required costs, and collect the reward you may only visit each one once per visit, but you may visit the same shop more than once in this phase. |
| At the start of the Village phase, fill each empty space in the open shops by taking chips of the matching tier from the bottom of that chip stack and placing them in the shops without looking at the face-down side. | **Curdy's Gear:** Gain any 2 tools of your choice for 1 gold, or 3 tools of your choice for 2 gold. |
| ## A VILLAGE TURN | **Healer's Hut:** Recover 1 health for free, and you may pay 1 gold to recover 1 additional health. The most you can heal from one visit is 2 health. |
| During a Village Turn you may perform one browse action, and then one shop action. | **Petter's Tavern:** Gain 2 gold and 1 Torch tool. |
| ## BROWSE ACTIONS | |
| You may choose one of the two browse actions: | ## BUYING A CHIP |
| **1. Peek at a Chip:** Look at the face-down side of a single chip in secret, then return it with its side of your choice face-up. <br><br>**Note:** You are not required to purchase this chip after peeking. | Choose one chip, pay the amount of gold shown on the price tag to the supply, and place the chip into your bag. You may look at both sides of the chip after purchasing it. |
| **2. Refresh a Shop:** Discard any tool to remove all the chips from the shop and refill them. Place the removed chips on top of an existing stack of their tier. | **Note:** Each Hero receives a discount on certain types of chips, but only if the face-up side matches the discounted type. |
| | If the purchased chip was the last chip in the shop, refill all empty spaces in every open shop in the shop you purchased from (don't discard any chips). Otherwise, do not refill empty shop spaces after making a purchase. |

---

## END OF THE VILLAGE PHASE

The Village Phase ends when each player has taken 3 turns.
