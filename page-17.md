# Village Turn Example

## Village Board

The Village board contains several shops and spaces that players can interact with during the Village phase.

### Board elements

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| **Tier 1 Shops** | Tier 1 shops available to players. |
| **Price Tag** | Indicates the price of a chip. |
| **Empty Chip Space** | An empty space that is refilled when a shop becomes completely empty. |
| **Tier 3 Shop** | The Tier 3 shop does not open until Round 2. |

## Village Turn Example

### Turn 1

Nyx starts their first Village turn with **11 Gold**.

They use their **Browse** action to look at the Tier 2 Gem chip and see a Coin on the reverse. They return the chip to its space with the Coin side facing up.

Nyx then uses their **Shop** action to buy the chip.

Nyx starts the game with the **Treasure Hound** skill, which gives them a discount of 1 Gold on Coin chips. Therefore, they pay **7 Gold**, leaving them with **4 Gold**.

It is then the next player's turn. Nyx does not refill the space from which they bought the chip.

---

### Turn 2

On their second turn, Nyx uses Browse to look at the Artifact chip, hoping to find another Coin that could provide a discount.

The chip is a **Shield**, so Nyx returns it to the space with the Artifact side facing up. This prevents other players from gaining that information.

For their Shop action, Nyx visits the **Healer's Hut**.

They recover **1 Health for free** and pay **1 Gold** to recover an additional Health, leaving them with **3 Gold**.

---

### Turn 3

For their final turn, Nyx looks at the Tier 2 Potion chip.

They cannot afford it, but the information may be useful during the Village phase of the next round.

Thanks to their discount, Nyx can buy the Chest chip from the Tier 1 shop.

Since a shop is now completely empty, all empty spaces on the Village board are refilled at the end of the turn.

The Tier 3 spaces are not refilled because that shop does not open until the next round.

---

# Game End

The game ends during the **Path Phase** when there are no more Path cards to reveal.

At the end of the game, players calculate their **Valor Points (VP)** as follows:

| Scoring | VP |
|---|---:|
| Each unlocked skill | 3 VP |
| Every 5 combined Health, XP and Gold | 1 VP |

### Example

Nyx has unlocked **3 skills**, so they gain:

**3 × 3 = 9 VP**

Nyx also has:

- **7 Gold**
- **4 XP**
- **2 Health**

Their combined total is:

**7 + 4 + 2 = 13**

For every 5 points, they gain 1 VP:

**13 ÷ 5 = 2 VP**, ignoring the remainder.

Therefore, Nyx gains **2 additional VP**.

---

## Determining the Winner

The player with the most **Valor Points (VP)** wins the game.

If two or more players are tied, the player with the most **Gold** wins the tie.

If the players are still tied after comparing Gold, the tied players **share the victory**.
